# Campus backdrop and app icons

`campus.jpg` is an illustrative campus generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project. It depicts an imaginary school and contains no school signage or official seal.

Prompt: "Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: widescreen 16:9 cover photo for a Philippine public secondary school inventory system login page. A real-looking but imaginary Philippines provincial public school courtyard in clear sunny morning light, with a handsome modest three-storey cream-and-white concrete classroom building, open walkways, blue or dark teal classroom doors, subtle warm yellow building trim, orderly garden beds and green lawn, tall leafy acacia trees at both outer sides. Editorial architectural photography from courtyard level at a slight angle. Welcoming, clean, and quiet natural setting. A centered website login card will cover the middle third of the image so the recognizably school-like building and trees should remain visible at both sides of the frame. Furnish subtle atmospheric depth, blue daytime sky. Fill the full frame with the photo. No person, no readable signage, no school name, no official emblems, no logos, no type, no watermark, no UI overlays."

The PNG icons use an original school-building pictogram drawn for this application.
